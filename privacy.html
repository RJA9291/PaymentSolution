3. Why I use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss possible work together.
  • To keep a record of our conversation if we go on to work together.

I process this data with your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the contact form, and, under UK GDPR, for my legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries. I do not use your data for marketing, and I do not make automated decisions about you.

4. Is it compulsory?

Your name, email address and message are needed for me to reply. If you would rather not use the form, you can email me directly. If you do not provide these details, I will not be able to respond to your enquiry.

5. Who I share it with

I never sell or rent your personal data. I only share it with these service providers, who help run this website:

  • Web3Forms delivers contact form messages to my email inbox.
  • Google (Gmail) stores the emails I receive.
  • Google Fonts and Unsplash supply the fonts and images on this page. Your browser connects to them directly, which shares your IP address with them.
  • Cloudflare and GitHub host this website.

Some of these providers store data outside Malaysia and the UK, including in the United States. I only use providers that protect personal data in line with the PDPA and UK GDPR. I may also disclose data if the law requires it.

6. How long I keep it

I keep enquiries for up to 12 months after our last contact, unless we start working together, in which case I keep them for as long as the work and any legal record-keeping require. After that, I delete them.

7. How I protect it

The form is sent over a secure, encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and my email account is protected with two-step verification. Only I can access your messages.

8. Your rights

You can ask me at any time to:

  • see the personal data I hold about you;
  • correct data that is wrong or out of date;
  • withdraw your consent, or limit how I use your data;
  • delete your data.

Just email japryatikah@gmail.com. I will reply within 21 days, as the PDPA requires. You may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Department of Malaysia (pdp.gov.my) or the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

9. Cookies

This website does not set any cookies or use tracking or analytics tools.

10. Changes to this notice

If I change this notice, I will update the date at the top of this page. If the English and Bahasa Melayu versions differ, the English version applies.
